AMBLE VISUAL POLISH V2
=======================

This package keeps the working API, login, product gallery, cart, checkout and
orders logic unchanged.

Visual improvements:
- More premium sticky navigation and controls
- Stronger hero depth and typography
- Elevated store-benefit cards
- Sticky, glass-like catalog filter toolbar
- Complete product-card surfaces with improved hover and focus states
- Better price and stock presentation
- Refined product gallery and sticky desktop product-information panel
- Cleaner form and authentication cards
- Elevated cart lines and order summary
- Cleaner checkout item cards and confirmation view
- Refined order-history cards, footer and toast notifications
- Improved mobile category scrolling and spacing
- Reduced-motion behavior preserved

The visual work is isolated in css/polish.css for easy rollback.
